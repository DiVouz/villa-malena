The villa

Space to breathe.
Views to remember.

Villa Malena is a spacious 130m² retreat set on five private acres, less than one kilometre from the traditional village of Glossa.

Designed for couples and families with children of all ages, the villa combines total privacy with open views across pine-covered hills, Skiathos and the Aegean. The infinity-style pool and broad terraces make sunsets part of every day.

Sleep & refresh

Three air-conditioned bedrooms - two double and one twin-share two bathrooms, including an upstairs jacuzzi bathroom. All bedrooms open onto a large shared veranda.

Pool & outdoors

Private outdoor pool, jacuzzi or hot tub, private garden, patio, balcony or terrace, roof terrace, BBQ, and sea and mountain views.

Living well

Generous shared spaces include a bright lounge and dining room with fireplace, air conditioning, central heating, Wi-Fi, free local telephone calls, TV, satellite TV, CD and DVD players.

Kitchen & practical

A fully equipped open-plan kitchen with cooker, fridge, freezer, microwave, kettle, toaster and dishwasher, plus a washing machine and iron.

Easy access

Private and secure parking, with Glossa within walking distance and Loutraki harbour four kilometres away.

Made for island living

Open, airy and connected to the landscape.

The ground floor brings together a light-filled lounge, dining room, bathroom and open-plan kitchen. It flows directly onto a covered terrace for breakfast and meals overlooking the sea.

Upstairs, two double bedrooms and one twin bedroom share a large veranda and panoramic outlook. High-quality finishes—including Italian wardrobes and cupboards—double-glazed windows and generous spaces make the villa comfortable in every season.
